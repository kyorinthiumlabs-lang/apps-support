Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 4, 2026
This shared policy applies to KyorinthiumLabs mobile apps unless an app has a separate app-specific privacy notice. It is written for App Store, Google Play, and user support pages.

1. Apps Covered

This policy covers mobile apps published by KyorinthiumLabs, including Who Am I? Guess Me!, Ben Kimim?, Blockforge, LinePath, AlfaBilgi, Arcana, AiStudio, AquaSort, CosmoBurst, Mosaica, and apps later added to the shared support site.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the app and features you use, we may process the following categories of information:

  • Account information such as email address, display name, avatar, and authentication identifiers.
  • Game and profile information such as scores, XP, levels, achievements, match history, leaderboard rank, and selected game modes.
  • Multiplayer information such as room codes, participant names, turn state, match results, and matchmaking status.
  • Technical information such as device type, operating system, app version, crash logs, diagnostics, and approximate network data.
  • Purchase information such as product identifiers, subscription status, transaction state, and platform purchase identifiers.
  • Support information such as messages, screenshots, account email, and details needed to resolve a request.

3. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

Our apps do not intentionally collect sensitive health data, precise GPS location, contact lists, SMS content, call logs, biometric data, or microphone/camera recordings unless a future feature clearly asks for permission and explains why it is needed.

4. How We Use Information

  • To create accounts and authenticate users.
  • To run gameplay, multiplayer rooms, matchmaking, scoring, and leaderboards.
  • To sync progress across devices where supported.
  • To verify purchases and subscriptions.
  • To prevent abuse, spam, cheating, and security incidents.
  • To diagnose crashes, fix bugs, and improve app performance.
  • To respond to support, privacy, and account deletion requests.

5. Third-Party Services

Our apps may use third-party services for authentication, database, hosting, analytics, crash reporting, purchases, and advertising. These may include:

  • Supabase for authentication, database, storage, and real-time app data.
  • Google Sign-In and Apple Sign in with Apple for authentication.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play for app distribution and purchases.
  • RevenueCat or similar purchase-management services where subscriptions or virtual products are used.
  • Google AdMob or similar ad services if ads are enabled in a future version.
  • Analytics and crash-reporting services when enabled to improve app stability.

6. Advertising ID

Some app versions may declare access to the advertising identifier so advertising or analytics can be added or supported. If personalized ads are enabled, platform settings may allow you to limit ad tracking or reset your advertising ID.

7. Sharing Information

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers that help operate the apps, process purchases, authenticate users, host data, detect abuse, or provide support. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect users, the apps, or our legal rights.

8. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the app, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, and maintain security. Account and gameplay data may be retained until deletion is requested or the account is removed.

9. Account Deletion and Data Requests

You may request account deletion, data access, or correction by opening a support request from the support page. Please include the app name and account email. We may ask for additional verification before fulfilling sensitive requests.

10. Children

Our apps are not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children where parental consent is required by law. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us through the support page so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

11. International Processing

Data may be processed in countries other than your own, depending on the infrastructure and third-party services used by the app. We use service providers that implement security and privacy safeguards appropriate to their role.

12. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect app data. No online service can guarantee absolute security, but we work to reduce risk through access controls, platform security features, and secure authentication providers.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as apps, services, laws, or platform requirements change. The updated date at the top shows when this policy was last revised.

14. Contact

For support, privacy questions, or account deletion requests, use the shared support page or email us at kyorinthiumlabs@gmail.com.
