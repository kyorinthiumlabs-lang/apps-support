FAQ

I cannot sign in with Google or Apple. What should I do?

Make sure the app is updated, restart the app, and try again. If the issue continues, send your platform, device model, app version, and a screenshot of the error.

How do I request account deletion?

Send an email with the app name and account email. We may ask you to confirm ownership before deleting data.

Which languages do you support?

You can contact us in any language. We primarily write in Turkish or English, but we use translation tools when needed so we can understand and respond to support requests from all users.

Where can I find the privacy policy?

The shared privacy policy is available at privacy.html. App-specific details are listed where relevant.
